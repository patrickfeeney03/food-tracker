## Backups and recovery

`food-tracker-backup` runs at 03:17 UTC daily. The private R2 bucket
`food-tracker-backups` has public access disabled and a 90-day expiry rule.
Both the CLI and scheduled Worker backups were downloaded from R2, restored
into disposable remote D1 databases, verified against exact application row
hashes and foreign keys, then the disposable databases were deleted.
